# 🎨 Canvas Recipe Builder

## ✅ **New Feature: Visual Recipe Creation**

A modern, canvas-style interface for creating recipes with drag-and-drop functionality and visual dish representation.

---

## 🎯 **Features**

### **1. Visual Ingredient Library**
- **Left Sidebar**: Browse ingredients by category
- **Search**: Quickly find ingredients
- **Drag & Drop**: Drag ingredients onto the canvas
- **Visual Cards**: Each ingredient has an icon and metadata

### **2. Interactive Dish Canvas**
- **Visual Canvas**: See your dish come together visually
- **Drag Ingredients**: Position ingredients on the plate
- **Move & Arrange**: Click and drag to reposition
- **Plate Visualization**: See a visual representation of your dish

### **3. Timeline Step Builder**
- **Visual Timeline**: Step-by-step cooking instructions
- **Add Steps**: Easy step addition
- **Reorder**: Move steps up or down
- **Rich Text**: Detailed step descriptions

### **4. Recipe Properties**
- **Right Sidebar**: Recipe metadata
- **Servings, Time, Difficulty**: Quick input
- **Nutrition Tracking**: Auto-calculated nutrition
- **Tags & Cuisine**: Organize your recipes

## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **Access the Canvas Builder**

1. **From Recipe Developer Page**:
   - Click the "🎨 Canvas Builder" button in the recipe header
   - Or navigate directly to `recipe-canvas.html`

2. **From Dashboard**:
   - Go to Recipe Developer
   - Click "Canvas Builder" button

### **Creating a Recipe**

1. **Add Ingredients**:
   - Search or browse ingredients in the left sidebar
   - Drag ingredients onto the canvas
   - Position them visually on the plate

2. **Build Steps**:
   - Click "Add Step" to create cooking instructions
   - Write detailed step descriptions
   - Reorder steps as needed

3. **Set Properties**:
   - Fill in servings, prep time, cook time
   - Select difficulty and cuisine
   - Add tags for organization

4. **Save Recipe**:
   - Click the floating "Save Recipe" button
   - Recipe is saved to localStorage
   - Compatible with existing recipe system
