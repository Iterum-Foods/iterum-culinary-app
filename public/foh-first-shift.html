FOH & bar — first shift

Staff quick guide for servers, hosts, bartenders, and runners.

Before your first shift

  1. Create your account at sign in.
  2. Open the shift app and tap Copy my user ID.
  3. Send your UID to your manager.
  4. After they add you, reopen the app and pick your workspace.
