      Crowd Manager (FP-250) — daily fire & building safety

      Required for nightclub, dancehall, discotheque, or bar per 527 CMR 1.00 §20.1.5.6.4. Complete on each day of operation prior to opening to patrons. Any No answer must be resolved before opening.

      Inspection items

      • Crowd managers appropriate for occupancy on the Certificate of Occupancy? (1 per 100–249 occupants; 1 per 250 thereafter.) Certificates of Successful Completion kept on site.
      • Employees trained to direct occupants to an exit in an emergency and the other duties in the emergency plan?
      • All egress paths maintained clear, unlocked, accessible?
      • All exit doors operable?
      • All exit sign lighting operable?
      • All emergency lighting operable?
      • Fire extinguishers in working order?
      • Exterior stairways and means of egress free of snow and ice?
      • Certificate of Inspection posted?
      • Sprinkler system inspected within the last year?
      • Fire alarm system inspected and tested within the last year?
      • Commercial cooking exhaust system cleaned at the required frequency?
      • Commercial cooking fire suppression system inspected in the last six months?
