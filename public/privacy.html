Privacy policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026. Applies to the Iterum web experience and the Iterum Shift mobile shell (Capacitor) that loads the same web content.

Who we are

Iterum provides software for restaurants and culinary teams to plan menus, recipes, shift workflows, and related operations. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our services.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up or sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (operated by Google) to process email and authentication credentials you provide.
  • Content and operational data you enter. Examples include workspace or project selections, menus, recipes, vendor notes, checklists, temperature or sanitizer logs, prep lists, and similar data you choose to save. Much of this is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and/or locally in your browser or device according to product behavior.
  • Technical data. Hosting providers (e.g. Vercel for the static web app) may process standard HTTP logs such as IP address, user agent, and request timestamps as part of delivering the service.

How we use information

  • To provide, secure, and improve the product.
  • To authenticate you and enforce workspace access rules configured for your account.
  • To respond to support requests when you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising in the product as described here; if that ever changes, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain consent.

Processors and subprocessors

We rely on industry-standard infrastructure, including but not limited to:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and related Firebase services) — see Google’s privacy documentation.
  • Vercel (or successor) for hosting the web application assets.

Retention

We retain account and workspace data as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account or specific data subject to our ability to verify your identity and any legal retention obligations.

Your choices

  • Access or update certain profile information through in-app settings where available.
  • Sign out of the app on shared devices.
  • Contact us to exercise privacy rights applicable in your jurisdiction (e.g. access, correction, deletion).

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adult professionals. They are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy where permitted by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: support@iterumfoods.com.
