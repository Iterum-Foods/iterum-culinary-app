# FOH & bar — first shift on Iterum (staff quick card)

**For:** Servers, hosts, bartenders, runners added to a workspace.  
**App:** `https://iterum-culinary-app.vercel.app/mobile-compliance.html` (add to home screen).  
**Web copy:** [foh-first-shift.html](../public/foh-first-shift.html) (same content, shareable link).  
**Full checklist:** [PHASE_1_TEAMMATE_FLOW_CHECKLIST.md](./PHASE_1_TEAMMATE_FLOW_CHECKLIST.md) (managers)

---

## Before your first shift

1. **Create account** — Same email your manager uses for invites (`/signin.html`).  
2. **Send your UID** — In the shift app, tap **Copy my user ID** and text it to your manager.  
3. **Wait for access** — Manager adds you in **Project hub** → refresh the app.  
4. **Pick workspace** — **Where are you working today?** must show your venue (not “No shared projects yet”).

---

## During service

| Job | Where in the app |
|-----|------------------|
| **Today’s priorities** | Home → **Today at a glance** (role chip shows FOH / Server / Bartender) |
| **Menu items** | **Menu** tab — published by kitchen/manager |
| **Drink specs** | **Bar** tab — manager-published builds |
| **Bar opening / closing** | **Bar** tab → checklists → **Done** / **Need** |
| **ID check (21+ / 18+)** | Top of **Home** or **Bar** — verify against physical ID |
| **Team message** | **Team log** — short posts managers see on dashboard |
| **86 / menu notes** | **Notes** tab → “The menu” |

---

## If something fails

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| No workspace in list | Confirm manager saved your UID to the **correct** project; sign out/in. |
| Empty menu | Ask manager to publish menu from office app (menu builder). |
| Permission errors | Screenshot message (no passwords); send to manager. |

---

## Managers

Copy invite text from **Project hub → Invite helper** (includes this flow).  
Role to assign: **Front of house** or **Kitchen / prep** or **Bartender** as appropriate.
