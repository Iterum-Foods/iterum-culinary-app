---

## What this is

Iterum is a **web app for professional kitchens**: recipes and menus with costing, a **Shift** phone layer for the floor (checks, temps, bar builds, how-to guides), vendors and **order guides**, and a bar-program pack (standards, specs, well pars).

You keep **your data**. We are not replacing your POS, accountant, or distributor portal.

## A typical week

- **Office:** update a recipe or menu; check costing; drop a vendor CSV/PDF into the price list and print an order guide.  
- **Bar / FOH:** published drink specs and how-to guides on the phone; opening/closing lists; ID date card.  
- **BOH:** temps and sanitizer on Shift (same logs the office dashboard can see).

---

## Pass / fail (so nobody argues later)

We call the window a **success** if:

1. You can sign in and staff can join the workspace.  
2. At least one menu, recipe pack, or bar pack is **saved and used** after the first week.  
3. Someone on the floor uses Shift **weekly** (checks, Bar tab, or How-to).  
4. No unresolved **severity-1** issue past the hours we agree in writing.

We **fail** if you withdraw, or data is lost without recovery, or (1)–(3) are not true at the end date.

## What we will **not** promise in this window

- POS, payroll, or accounting integration  
- Automated emails to distributors  
- Perfect PDF extraction from every price book (we review a table; CSV is more reliable)  
- Calling a workspace a “location” in reports until site IDs exist  
- App Store / Play Store apps as the primary product (web + installable Shift page)

## How we talk about numbers
