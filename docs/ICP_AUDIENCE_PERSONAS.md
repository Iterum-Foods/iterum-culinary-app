| They need | How Iterum answers |
|-----------|-------------------|

## Persona A — Small business owner / operator

**Who:** Owner or GM of an independent or small-group restaurant; wears many hats; may not have a dedicated compliance manager.

**Worries**

- Health inspections, HACCP gaps, temperature logs — **“one bad week and we’re in trouble.”**
- **Cannot afford** large compliance platforms, consultants, or enterprise kitchen stacks priced for chains.
- Vendors and legacy tools **hold their data hostage** — hard to export, switch, or audit.

**Jobs to be done**

- Run daily ops with confidence (logs, checklists, team on the floor).
- Prove compliance when asked — organized records by day and workspace.
- Keep a copy of business-critical data **they control** (backup off-device).

| Pillar | Role for this persona |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Run the shift** | Primary — dashboard, mobile shift app, team |
| **Archive** | Primary — trust: backups, exports, audit trail |
| **Develop** | Secondary — menu/recipe when they or the chef update the offer |

**Message hooks:** *“Compliance your size.”* · *“Your logs, your export.”* · *“Not priced like the chains.”*

## Persona B — Restaurant / chef launching or relaunching

**Who:** Chef-owner, executive chef, or culinary lead opening a new concept, seasonal menu, or second room — needs **launch-ready** documentation.

**Worries**

- Recipes scattered across notes, photos, and old employers’ systems.
- Menu not **systemized** — costing, vendors, and line setup slip until opening week.
- Opening with chaos instead of a **single source** the team can follow.

**Jobs to be done**

- Build and cost the menu before doors open.
- Tie recipes, ingredients, and vendors to **one workspace** for launch.
- Snapshot and archive the “opening menu” so it’s recoverable and shareable with the team.

| Pillar | Role for this persona |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Develop** | Primary — recipes, menu builder, vendors, ingredients |
| **Archive** | Primary at launch — export menu bundle, backup before go-live |
| **Run the shift** | Ramps after open — HACCP, checklists, mobile |

**Message hooks:** *“Launch with the menu organized.”* · *“From R&D to service in one workspace.”*

---

## Persona C — Cook building a career recipe portfolio

**Who:** Line cook, sous, or chef de partie (or aspiring chef) who wants **their** recipes saved and portable across jobs — personal IP and craft history.

**Worries**

- Years of work **lost** when leaving a job (no export, employer-owned systems only).
- No **system** — everything in phone notes or paper.
- Hard to show growth (portfolio) when interviewing or pitching a pop-up.

**Jobs to be done**

- Save, tag, and version personal recipes in one place.
- Export or backup so the portfolio **moves with them**.
- Optionally mirror a workplace workspace when the employer uses Iterum.

| Pillar | Role for this persona |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Develop** | Primary — recipe library & developer |
| **Archive** | Primary — career-long backup & per-project exports |
| **Run the shift** | Optional — if they lead station checks or use employer workspace |

**Message hooks:** *“Your recipes, your career.”* · *“Organized today, portable tomorrow.”*
