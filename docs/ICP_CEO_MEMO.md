## Three segment options (choose one primary)

| Segment | Who | Why us now | Risk if we over-rotate |
|---------|-----|------------|-------------------------|
| **A — Multi-unit operator** | Small group (2–8 venues), shared procurement | Shared vendors + costing across “projects” matches roadmap | Product still segments on `projectId`; must not **call** it “location” in decks until site IDs ship |
| **B — Single high-end venue** | One kitchen, deep menu/R&D | Fastest path to wow on recipes, menu costing, FOH tools | Less pressure to finish multi-site; may under-test teammate/membership flows |
| **C — Culinary consultant / multi-client** | One lead, many client workspaces | Natural fit for **project** = client | Pricing and support load scale with client count; need clear seat/workspace packaging |
